# v0.5.6

## Highlights

- `expose` grants now survive a restart. A daemon saves its grants to `<key>.grants.json`, next to its peers file, and loads them when it starts, before any peer connects. Before, every restart dropped them, so peers were offered no ports until someone ran `expose` again.
- On macOS, `pai-sho daemon install` waits for the old service to stop before starting the new one. Before, reinstalling over a running daemon could fail with "Bootstrap failed: 5: Input/output error" and leave pai-sho stopped.
- The app reports its version as 0.5.6, matching pai-sho.
